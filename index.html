03 / SELECTED WORK

Projects

Multiparticle model of olefin polymerisation

Problem
Polymer properties depend on a particle morphology that cannot be measured during reaction, so its effect on rate and molecular weight could not be quantified.
Approach
Built the growing particle as an assembly of mesoparticles of distributed size, coupling mass transfer and kinetics, and extended it from batch to a continuous reactor using Monte Carlo sampling of the residence time distribution.
Result
[Add the number here.] Access to quantities previously out of reach for predicting polymer properties and optimising reactor operation. Published in Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. (2026).
